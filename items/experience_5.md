## Working at an Escape Room 
I worked at an escape room as a game master and administrative assistant.

I worked here from December 2024 - July 2025

I had met two of my favorite people at that job. Although the actual work environment was not pleasant, the two girls made it bearable to be there everyday.

One of the few moments i enjoyed in that building was having  my family come by one of the games. They failed so terribly but it was incredibly funny watching them and providing them hints

The manager didn’t know how to do anything. Without zero exaggeration, I would constantly have myself manage everything while he sat beside me on the phone. He would ask me for information on his own business. I essentially managed the store and everything in it while being paid less than the one male co-worker at the time. Finding out in real time that he pays the girls less and the men more was infuriating.

**Grade: F**
