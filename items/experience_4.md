## Working at Playdium
I worked as a Cashier at the Cineplex owned arcade as the covid lockdown began to lift. 

I worked here from July 2021 - August 2023

The staff was required to be 18+ due to the handling and distribution of alcohol. This meant I was lucky enough to have co-workers that were all around my age. I had met long term friends and my long term partner while employed there.

With working in an arcade, there will be prizes. To keep track of the prizes, Managers and occasionally regular employees would arrive early (6am-1pm) or late (8pm - 3am) to count every single prize in the building. I was picked for an early count shift with my favourite manager which resulted in us counting for one hour and playing games for the remainder of the shift.

Over 70 arcade games going off all at once was lovely for my headaches. Sometimes they would also try and convince employees to stay far past their shift end “to help them out”. It often led to working 9-13+ hour days when you anticipate a 5-7 hour shift.

**Grade: C**
