## Volunteer for Community Day at Cineplex

I volunteered to work at Cineplex for their Community Day event. This event offers free admissions to movies and discounted food items with the intention of making the movie experience accessible to all.
I did this back in 2024.

The intention behind the event was great. It provided an opportunity for everyone from all financial backgrounds to have a fun experience without breaking the bank.

At the location I was at (Windsor), they had face painters and balloon artists in the lobby. All of the coworkers I had at the time all got “mystery” face paint. Only after the facepainting was completed we were allowed to see what was on our face. I had no idea I was going to be Spongebob for the remainder of my shift but it was fun!

We had a large turnout which was great but cleaning the theatres afterwards was a nightmare.

**Grade: B**
