## Backstage for School Musical

I attended an arts school in high school so that meant A LOT of productions over the school year. I had the opportunity to help backstage with the main production of Camp Rock: The Musical.

This took place from November 2018-April 2019.

I had easily completed my volunteer hour requirements with just the backstage help but I had also made some solid friendships from this experience

There was a time where the backstage crew and the main actors swapped positions on stage for rehearsal. When the curtains opened, I had the opportunity to say a few lines as Mitchie Torres (Demi Lovato) in the play. The director did not find it as funny as we all did.

We had all gotten yelled at for wasting her time with this shenanigans

**Grade: A**
