## Learning Haitian Creole on Duolingo

I downloaded duolingo to bridge the language barrier between my cousins and myself. They all grew up in Montreal, Quebec, making French their first language. Although French is spoken the most, my family typically converses in Haitian Creole due to our heritage.

I tried this out in October 2022 up until November 2023

I enjoyed the stage where I was starting to pick up on particular words my mother would say over the phone. It also felt great actively learning something new. 

There was a moment when my mom got off the phone with my aunt anf forgot to “switch” back over to English. It's important to note that she has done this all my life, so this was nothing new. The only thing that changed was that I partially understood her.

I didnt particularly enjoy having to maintain my duolingo streak. I ultimately gave up when I accidentally lost my streak of 392 day. You will be missed.

**Grade: C**
